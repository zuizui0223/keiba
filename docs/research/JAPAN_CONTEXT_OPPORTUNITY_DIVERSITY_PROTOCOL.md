# Historical race-supply balance and information substitution

2026-11-24. Follow-up to completed PR89. The within-horse own-update term is clearly negative in the two shorter bands, but weaker in the longer band. Raw distance contrasts do not identify an environment-diversity mechanism. New hypothesis: a more balanced observed supply of two surfaces within a horse's historical venue-season portfolio is associated with stronger substitution of fixed family corrections by own information.

## Fixed observational design

Use exact A/B source hashes and pre-2022 identity, circuit and strict classified gates. Preserve PR89's three bands and common 2022/24 horse counts (531/599/267), original first-seed update means, and existing priors/profile formulas. Per band, retain horses observed in both 2022 and 2024 and calculate Y=-2*d*(u2024-u2022), the exact change in incremental MSE gain from own-history update with source-A family correction d fixed. Negative = diminished additional value. The 2024 target surface fraction is not part of Y; observed 2024 membership is still a selection criterion. This is a predictive model-input outcome, not a biological learning measure.

Supply proxy uses only distinct classified JRA races in 2017–21, by recorded venue × calendar quarter × distance band. Each cell with at least 10 races has turf share p and balance q_cell=4p(1-p), zero for one-surface supply, one at equal supply. Horse portfolio = relative counts of its earlier JRA flat starts across venue-quarter cells at **any** eligible distance in 2017–21. Weighted mean of supported q_cell values, renormalize over supported portfolio weight only if >=80% covered; otherwise HOLD that row. Count each race once, not once per runner. Weighted average race count in supported cells is separate supply-volume control. No B labels, future venues or held-year supply used to define balance. Recorded race supply and selected past venue mix are NOT individual eligibility/availability or an exogenous treatment. Race class, trainer, ownership and scheduling may confound associations.

Primary seed 20261124: pooled matched horse×band rows, weighted so each distinct horse contributes total weight one; regression Y on q/0.1, distance-band fixed effects, log1p prior eligible start count, log1p added own starts, |fixed baseline−0.5| and log1p race supply. Negative balance coefficient is hypothesized. Fixed controls are descriptive, not causal; all fits remain conditional on support and supplied history. Resample entire sire blocks across bands (999 refits), require >=100 horses, >=30 sires and >=100 rows in each band, full rank, numerical condition and >=95% valid draws for pointwise CI. Retain all rows except predeclared coverage exclusions; report complete vs supported counts. Never select favorable bands or seeds. Sensitivities: band-only adjustment; equal-weighted supported venue-quarter cells; correction-energy magnitudes (|d|,|delta u|,|d delta u|) added to controls; same-horse fixed-effects on horses supported in >=2 bands with unchanged gates. Report all three fixed seeds. If within-horse design or other fit lacks support, HOLD without relaxation. Report d RMS, delta-u RMS and cosine alignment by band as descriptive exact scale-vs-alignment diagnostic, not an inference about between-band differences.

The analysis is post-hoc relative to the already inspected PR89 outcomes, not an untouched prospective test. Dependence across bands, seeds, observed histories and deeper pedigree remains. Do not infer causal adaptation, actual feasible habitat choice, heritability, individual learning, survival, or market/wagering outcomes. Only anonymous aggregates are retained.
