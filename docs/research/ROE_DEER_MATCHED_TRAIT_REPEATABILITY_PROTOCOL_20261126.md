# Same-deer, same-period trait comparison of residual repeatability and genomic information

26 November 2026. Secondary hypothesis motivated by observed POH positive, SPEED HOLD, and road sensitivity. Frozen before inspecting overlap counts, matched fits, or trait repeatability. Use source-locked Dryad 2020 v1, exact ANIMAL + recorded YEAR/MONTH cell intersection (not true chronology); original POH rules and SPEED finite-cell averaging of metadata-concordant duplicates. Require exact AGE/SEX concordance between endpoints; report disagreements and drop them from both arms. Eligible cohort consists of deer with >=4 shared concordant cells, >=100 deer or HOLD without outcome fits. Same animals, donor split, target and nested 0/1/3 masks, design, shrinkage 3, signed GRM ridge 1 for both outcomes. Donors and focal animals are restricted to eligible shared cohort (different from PR98/99, not expected to reproduce their numerical effects). Donor-only equal-animal-weighted outcome centering/scaling separately for each trait before fitting; compare dimensionless squared losses. No clipping, road conditioning, retuning, transformation search or raw-value export.

Residual repeatability: for each focal deer across all its matched cells, compute mean residual, mean squared residual and mean distinct ordered residual-pair product after cross-fit donor covariate prediction. Across deer use equal-animal-weighted (E pair product − E residual squared mean)/(E residual square − E residual mean squared), where subtraction in numerator and denominator is (E residual)^2; correlation-like repeatability, not a fitted random-intercept ICC. Residuals used for descriptive repeatability include held-out targets, never for training their own predictions. Three prespecified POH-minus-SPEED contrasts: higher residual repeatability, higher initial standardized genomic MSE gain, more negative 3−0 gain change. 999 conditional paired-deer bootstrap draws, central 98.333% percentile intervals (three Bonferroni contrasts), fixed predictions; primary seed 20261124 and two shared-source sensitivity splits 20261125/26. Report endpoints even when joint directional prediction fails; no selective choice of seeds or claims of equivalence. No label permutation in this contrast-only diagnostic. Even if all directions agree, two traits cannot establish a repeatability moderator law, mechanism, causal genetic effect, or independence from shared spatial environments; road context remains a separate sensitivity. Negative/uncertain results retained. No raw records or IDs redistributed.
