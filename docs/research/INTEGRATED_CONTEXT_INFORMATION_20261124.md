# Experience-dependent value of family information in environmental allocation

2026-11-24 · Research synthesis, observational and retrospective; not submitted.

## Central question and claim boundary

When can recorded family histories help predict the realized environmental allocation of a competitive animal, and how does their additional value change when individual history accumulates? The system is managed Thoroughbred racing. The strongest outcome here is the observed fraction of eligible JRA turf starts (or the first observed surface within a year), conditional on archive inclusion and classified starts. It is not physiological fitness, voluntary habitat preference, genetic causation, official debut, or a measurement of an animal using family information. Do not pool these endpoints with relative finishing ranks or Australian GPS timing.

## Main findings: four connected levels

1. **Observation structure.** PR83: among 4,187 supported 2021 horses, paternal/maternal distinct historical donor means 190.657/2.078; after equalizing to 2.071 per side, the directional MSE gap drops from 0.0089821 to 0.0012579 (86.0% arithmetic reduction). Both equal-count channels individually improve the count-adjusted model in six repeated conditions, both separate pointwise cluster intervals positive. Their primary difference is uncertain, with a reversal in one seed. Counts are not genetic contribution, independence or equal precision.
2. **Allocation versus response.** PR81: earlier family occupancy predicts annual observed surface fraction after own history (2021 7,276 horses, RMSE 0.423246→0.405495), positive conditional pointwise intervals in 24/24 sensitivities. PR82 first eligible annual surface after history-rich baseline and donor count, RMSE 0.403959→0.386565; later use conditional on first surface 0.318305→0.315012. For 2,150 raw-first-record horses 0.446373→0.406045. This is not official debut. PR80 paired same-horse rank-residual difference transfer, 985 2021 horses, gain +0.0008060 MSE [-0.0013482,0.0027639]; 4/12 positive estimates, all intervals include zero. These different endpoints/support cannot be directly compared as effect sizes. The contrast motivates an allocation–performance decoupling hypothesis, not proof of decoupling or heritability. PR78 family-indexed context resemblance was positive, but PR79 within-horse support was insufficient and correspondence split-sensitive. PR84 same-management comparison has only 716 matched horses (9.8% of primary cohort), main P−K interval crosses zero; raw-first subset favorable but selected.
3. **Conditional predictive information.** PR77 off-context block descriptively helps history-poor novel context; diversity interaction itself fails. PR85 fixed-source transfer in anchored continuing horses 2022–24: all 36 useful-information intervals cross zero; all matched other-sire intervals positive. Do not mistake beating a worse placebo for useful improvement beyond own baseline. PR86 cold 2022 subgroup (334/3,011) MSE gain +0.0197803 [0.0040655,0.0343879]; all three seed intervals positive. In 2024 refreshed own+global baseline RMSE 0.317934 vs full family correction 0.332244 (MSE gain −0.0093043, all three intervals negative). PR87 source-A-only count-calibrated family correction yields 2022/23 gains over no-family baseline in all seeds, and beats full/constant correction across 15 distinct later conditions. Main 2024 refreshed gain +0.0022820 [−0.0017150,0.0065175] is uncertain. Weight monotonicity is imposed, not a biological law. New test uses earlier fitting records only but is post-hoc relative to previously inspected B outcomes.
4. **Population aggregation.** PR86 two-stratum A2021 versus B2022 gain difference: 79.8–89.1% arithmetic composition term, comparing different archives/years with different coverage. PR88 fixed same-sire correction on 599 common 2022/24 horses: gain +0.0117119→−0.0158656. Own-update contribution −0.0161058 [-0.0250567,−0.0077816], observed allocation shift −0.0114717, total −0.0275775. Membership remainder +0.0104967 partly offsets the decline. All 18 overlapping pair/intersection sensitivities have negative own-update intervals and positive membership remainders. Membership is observed sample inclusion, not survival. The PR86 and PR88 composition terms are different estimands and cannot be compared as if contradictory.

## Exact analytical bridge

For a baseline B and a family correction d, outcome Y, squared-loss gain is g=2(Y−B)d−d². Let u be a change in B computed from earlier own history, holding d fixed. Then g_new−g_old=−2du. This separates predictor overlap from an observed outcome change without identifying a causal learning effect. Across the same horses and a fixed earlier baseline, the temporal change is 2d(Y_late−Y_early)−2d(u_late−u_early). The population average adds a membership remainder if the two evaluation sets differ.

The best interpretation is experience-dependent **predictive** information substitution. Family signal magnitude, alignment with residual error, and available own information all matter. A constrained source-A calibration can improve a misspecified full correction; optimal Bayesian information acquisition need not be harmful. The finite fitted correction can worsen MSE if its weight is too large. The data cannot distinguish genes from common management, breeding allocation, or other shared environments, or certify multigeneration pedigree/genomic relationships.
