## S2. Earlier signal and negative controls

The off-context analysis (PR77) showed descriptive gain from an additional block of features on 40,130 novel-supported starts / 5,729 horses (RMSE 0.318950→0.317989), but the predeclared diversity interaction gain was −0.232×10⁻⁶ MSE and the conservative eight-comparison interval was [−25.987,+25.523]×10⁻⁶. The extra block is not an isolated genetic or diversity effect.

PR78 measured family-indexed surface-specific resemblance using separated horses and days: main equal-sire mean product +0.006531 [0.003626,0.009884] on 83 sires and 174 sire-years; trainer-disjoint main +0.005304 [0.001279,0.009781] on 57 sires and 105 sire-years. Four of six pointwise intervals positive. Annual estimates are heterogeneous. PR79's within-horse support was insufficient and its alternative-sire comparisons split-sensitive. PR80 paired performance transfer was unresolved (985 horses; gain +0.000806, interval includes zero). These results motivate the distinct managed allocation endpoint; they do not establish genetic reaction norms.

PR81 annual occupancy and PR82 first/later observed allocation are separate outcomes. The first raw observed record subgroup in PR82 (2,150 horses) had RMSE 0.446373→0.406045; zero eligible earlier-year history and archive-first are not official debut. The main first/later MSE point estimates must not be used as a formal difference of effects. PR83 uses a count-adjusted first-surface baseline, not the PR81 annual model; full gap 0.008982 and equal-count gap 0.001258 are from the supported 4,187-horse matched evaluation. Both parent cluster intervals for the equalized directional gap are uncertain.

PR84 same-trainer/age/sex/year matched same-sire versus other-parent contrast: 716/7,276 horses, 80 sires, 154 trainers. Baseline-to-same-sire gain +0.004745 with separate positive sire and trainer pointwise intervals. The stricter matched other-parent minus same-sire gain +0.004100 has intervals spanning zero; a raw-first-record subset of 209 horses gives +0.014234 with positive pointwise intervals. Selection and observed management balance do not remove all shared environment.

## S3. Chronology, observation and calibration

PR85's frozen profiles give no positive useful-gain interval among 36 tested conditions; matched alternative-sire superiority does not imply improvement over own-history baseline. In PR86, the A2021/B2022 symmetric two-stratum contrast has an arithmetic composition fraction of 79.8–89.1% across seeds. It changes archives and years and is not the same as the PR88 membership term. Separate factorial history/profile updates show that full correction may worsen the refreshed no-family baseline; main 2024 gain −0.009304 [−0.016082,−0.001845]. PR87 estimates history-dependent weights using earlier A only, opposite focal half and fixed bins. The fitted score is distinct from the outcome-dependent scalar optimum. All 15 later full-vs-gate and pooled-vs-gate pointwise intervals favor the gate, while late no-family-vs-gate intervals remain uncertain. PR93 keeps the matched Jan1–Mar27 window in all evaluated years; 2022/23 gains supported in all seeds, 2024/25 not. The 6.83% 2025 identity anchor coverage prevents a whole-population extrapolation.

The common-cohort PR88 update contrast is a fixed-predictor arithmetic estimand, not an intervention on animal experience. The membership remainder compares full versus intersection means, not survival, demographic recruitment or causal selection. The pointwise sire-resampling intervals do not give a confidence interval for the membership remainder.

## S4. Environmental and recorded-identity boundaries

PR89 short and reference-band paired own-update effects have negative intervals in 12/12 sensitivities per band; longer band 9/12, with the primary longer interval spanning zero. Long-band membership can amplify rather than offset the decline. These bands share records and differ in support, surface mixture and profile magnitude. PR91's historical supply-balance proxy (1,389 horse×band rows, 1,191 horses, 158 sires) has a primary adjusted slope +0.000441 per 0.1 balance with interval crossing zero, all 12 fitted intervals cross zero, and the within-horse fit fails the frozen short-band count gate (86<100). The proxy is not an individual opportunity set.

PR92 swaps correction direction while retaining magnitude; it neither defines genomic nonkin nor removes management. ## S5. Heterogeneity, observation-membership nonreplication and direct-source parity

The bounded common weight is clip(A/E,0,1) with positive energy E. For a partition, H is the excess of two-stratum sample-optimal gain over pooled sample-optimal gain; a nonnegative H is expected from nesting, not a predictive result. Its algebraic expression is an energy-weighted variance of unconstrained stratum ratios minus a clipping adjustment. See [derivation, 207 arithmetic checks and aggregate values](INFORMATION_WEIGHT_BOUNDARY_AND_HETEROGENEITY_20261125.md).

A shorter 2022–23 window after observing these counts would be a separate post-hoc test and is not needed to establish the principal longitudinal result.

## S6. Cross-program comparison, not pooled evidence

The earlier Australian GPS panel demonstrates source-conditional horse-ID recurrence and better own-history prediction but lacked same-horse cross-horizon support in Doomben. The separate Japanese rank panel shows much smaller family increments and a 2026 post-hoc refresh comparison favoring own-history updating. A 5,163-horse rank-gate analysis gives a within-cohort change −0.000105406 (interval includes zero) and a membership-amplified full change −0.000182929, contrasting the offset in allocation. These endpoints and contrasts cannot be pooled. The [membership nonalignment crosswalk](INFORMATION_MEMBERSHIP_NONALIGNMENT_20261125.md) and [repository-wide review](REPOSITORY_WIDE_INFORMATION_DYNAMICS_SYNTHESIS_20261125.md) retain the prior rank, GPS, partial pedigree, anonymous SNP, human market and generative-model studies. The datasets do not identify the same genome-to-phenotype-to-market pathway. Do not add betting predictions or simulated ABM/PDE outcomes to empirical claims.

## S8. Prospective and publication gates

No future claims have been evaluated. [PR94](https://github.com/zuizui0223/keiba/pull/94) is a fictional-only software rehearsal and prospective protocol, not data acquisition or a real pledge. A stable authorized parent registry, timestamped predictors, denominator including unknown-parent/never-observed horses and independently frozen future predictions remain necessary. Upstream publication permissions, ethics/data-use statement, authorship, archive-release identifier and journal requirements are unresolved. The main result is observational predictive redundancy, not genetic adaptation. Do not keep adding retrospective gates to seek positive intervals in the already inspected sources.
