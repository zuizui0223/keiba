# Repeated competition: do pair-specific historical residuals transfer?

26 November 2026. Horse-only research track. Secondary question following prior GPS, rank and allocation studies, frozen before inspecting repeated-dyad outcomes/support. Source A SHA-locked 2010–2021 archive with unknown publisher license; no raw redistribution, no wagers. Existing prepared 2,092,836 records/90,962 horses and 2019–21 JRA 1600–1999m 57,208 starts/15,129 horses must reproduce. All JRA classified earlier starts for broad own rank prior, same band/surface earlier starts for context prior and recorded-sire profile. Do not drop horses because parent missing; missing family score is neutral. A race is a complete classified order under existing preparation rules, not certified complete entry census. No same-year information in predictors.

Orient each unordered pair lexically by stable source horse ID. Outcome 1 if A finishes behind B. Build five antisymmetric feature differences: broad own normalized rank mean (5-start shrink to .5), surface-specific middle-band own mean (5-start shrink to broad), other-horse same-sire/context mean (8-horse shrink to .5, exclude focal), log1p broad and context earlier counts /4. Fit race-equal logistic ridge with penalty .001 on mean logistic loss, no intercept, 2017–18 calibration only. For 2019 and 2020 donor and 2020/21 target years use frozen coefficients and prior-only input. All donor rows strictly earlier than target. N=base; H=N+earlier signed horse residual means (average by horse-race, shrink 5 starts), subtract B from A. D=H+same-surface same-dyad mean residual after subtracting horse residual terms, shrink k/(k+3), zero for unsupported pairs. Clip probabilities to [0,1] for Brier evaluation, no tuning; unclipped contrast sensitivity. Compare H vs D on same target pairs. Main 2021 prior >=1 same-surface meeting, >=500 distinct surface dyads, >=60 days, >=100 horses. 2020 and >=2 prior meetings and all-pair coverage secondary. Equal race weight among selected pairs; earlier residual per horse-race weight equal; fixed 999 race-day block conditional resamples for 95% interval. Retain all adverse/support-HOLD results. Repeated encounters selected and outcomes dependent; conditional intervals omit other crossed dependence. Not a causal pair interaction, tactical advantage, physiological adaptation or independent prospective score. Not a betting model. If positive, next use non-kin/kin and same-trainer controls and matched alternative opponent identity; if uncertain, retain boundary. No source raw records, IDs or individual predictions uploaded.
