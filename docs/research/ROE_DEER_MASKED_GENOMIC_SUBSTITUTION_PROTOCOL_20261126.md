# Wild roe deer independent-species information-depth test

26 November 2026. Frozen before reading outcome values or fit results. Structural source audit [PR97](https://github.com/zuizui0223/keiba/pull/97) has established 2,288 POH records / 256 deer and exact labeled GRM matches. This is a new analysis of public real observations, not a new collection or a fully untouched sample relative to the source authors' published results.

**Estimand:** Masked-record prediction, not a chronological forecast. YEAR may encode capture year. Treat recorded YEAR+MONTH as a cell label only. Use POH (daytime fraction in open habitat) in [0,1]. Require finite outcome and recorded covariates; average duplicate animal-period cells only when AGE/SEX agree, otherwise HOLD. Retain the same one target and nested 0/1/3 other-record history depths on each deer (at least four distinct eligible period cells). Minimum 100 evaluated deer. Counts/outcomes and source hashes must reproduce. No use of all-period habitat-similarity matrices or future target in focal histories.

**Training:** Deterministic three seeds 20261124 (primary), 20261125/26. Two disjoint animal halves; each target uses other-half donors only. On donor cells fit ridge (penalty 1 except intercept), month×sex and age categorical baseline with equal total animal weights. Predict focal target and its other records using those coefficients. Reveal other-record residuals in fixed outcome-blind random order; update baseline by sum(residual)/(n+3). From donor mean residual per animal construct fixed genomic correction d=G_FD(G_DD+I)^−1 residual_D. Check exact IDs, symmetry, finite values and positive shifted matrix; Cholesky solve. Original GRM is slightly indefinite, so do not call it a covariance estimate or use unregularized inverse. Keep signed G entries; fixed lambda=1 chosen before fitting, not tuned on target labels. No clipping in primary squared-loss identity; separate clipped sensitivity.

**Primary contrasts:** gain G0 from adding the fixed genomic correction with no own revealed records, and G3−G0 as 3 own records become available on exactly the same target. Verify per-deer difference equals −2du with own baseline update u. Directional expectation G0>0 and G3−G0<0; report all point estimates at 0/1/3 and no-family vs full RMSE. Conditional paired deer bootstrap 999 draws and 97.5% percentile intervals for two contrasts; not robust to all genetic/spatial dependence or fitting/selection, so not confirmatory population intervals. Additional 199 label permutations within recorded sex (fixed fold, donor residuals, outcome masks; same genomic geometry), report true versus label-swap distributions for G0 and G3−G0. Exchangeability of genomes across space is not established; tail fractions descriptive, not causal p-values. No threshold or seed selection after results.

**Interpretation:** if a useful genomic correction also becomes redundant as own observations are revealed, this is an independent-species and distinct dataset analog of the horse predictive-information result. It would not demonstrate time/age dependence, shared environment independence, genotype causal effects or voluntary preference. If G0 is uncertain or unfavorable, do not promote a sign of −2du alone to useful information substitution. Negative/null and support HOLD preserved. Actual chronology, spatial opportunity and family-versus-environment partition await source semantic audit (including 2022 revision choice). No raw records or IDs uploaded.
