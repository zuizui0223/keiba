# Initial popularity counterfactual under uncertain private evidence

27 November 2026 JST. Synthetic theory, no claim of fitted market or betting prediction. Eight individuals, fixed latent log-strength theta, focal A/B equal at .35; initial count 20/8 swapped, other counts unchanged; mass 80. 60 batches × 12 equal-weight agents; within each batch popularity is fixed at pre-batch state. Utility = 2 m_i + alpha log(count_i) + iid standard Gumbel; independent private observations x=theta+sigma Z, Z standard normal. In the primary posterior variant m=x/(1+sigma^2), normal prior mean zero variance one; raw variant m=x is deliberately unshrunk sensitivity. All worlds/parameters share same Gaussian and Gumbel shocks within each of 256 independent seeded synthetic replicates; separate fixed draws per replicate. Alpha is net popularity response, not identified imitation vs price response. Grid alpha −.5,0,.5,1,1.25,1.5 and sigma .25,1,3 for both evidence mappings. No ability-information-dependent social coefficient, no intervention on underlying ability. Main terminal intervention D=((sA+−sB+)−(sA−−sB−))/2; mechanical no-feedback retention D0=(20−8)/(80+720)=.015, exact. Primary uncertainty contrast D(sigma=3)−D(sigma=.25) at alpha .5,1,1.5, show all alpha values and raw sensitivity; not posthoc sign choice. Additional outcomes total variation, initial favorite retention, squared distance to synthetic latent softmax reference. Unequal focal gap .3 diagnostic only: latent-better A starts less popular in swapped world; compare terminal A>B frequency (ties half). Does not describe actual race success or financial outcome. MC95 normal intervals of replicate means with paired differences; not real-world uncertainty or multiplicity-adjusted inference. QA mass, zero-feedback retention, identical world and deterministic reproducibility. No market calibration, no validated sigma from horse-history uncertainty, no actual quotes or participants. PR76 has one aligned two-timepoint case, no stable cross-date horse ID link; PR118 fixed-target own-score rollback is empirical fitted-model evidence but not evidence for market causality. Keep both evidence mappings and all adverse signs. No modification to existing betting or frozen score ledgers.
