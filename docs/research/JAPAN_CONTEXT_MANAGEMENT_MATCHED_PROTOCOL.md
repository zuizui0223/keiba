# Management-matched same-sire vs other-parent information

2026-11-24. Parallel follow-up to PR82 while PR83 count-equalization is running. Freeze before new scores. PR82 reproduced a family-associated allocation signal even after prior trainer and entry demographics, but a shared trainer can transmit information beyond a pooled historical trainer mean. This diagnostic asks if within the same recorded management context, direct same-sire donors predict first observed environment better than equal numbers of donors sharing neither recorded direct parent.

## Design

Source A SHA, exact original denominator, annual 7,265/7,381/7,276 horses, PR82 C-F primary parity and B primary RMSE parity. First eligible observed JRA turf/dirt 1600–1999m in each year; not official debut. Use unchanged PR82 learned no-family B, prior year features, as-recorded entry metadata, opposite horse half fit. Compute historical out-of-year B residuals in 2018–2020 for 2020/2021 evaluation. For each target, donors are latest earlier eligible horse-year in opposite horse half, distinct horses, known direct parents, trainer, age and sex. Donor pairs match exactly on recorded entry trainer equal to target's entry trainer, target age category (2,3,4,5+) and sex (recorded male/female/gelding), same donor year. Same-sire donor must have different recorded dam; other-parent donor shares neither direct recorded parent. Deterministic outcome-blind SHA ranking without replacement within target, cap eight pairs. Across targets donors may be reused. No current outcomes enter features. Do not impute unknown metadata or use older record to substitute for missing latest metadata.

Base M0 = B plus historical global residual mean, clipped. K and P add corresponding mean-centered paired-donor sums divided by n+8 (same n on both sides), clip. No-support targets K=P=M0 retained in all denominator. Primary 2021 first seed, matched targets, P-K squared-error gain. Require >=100 targets and >=30 clusters for intervals; separate sire and recorded trainer bootstrap, 999 draws. Report M0-K as useful information criterion (beating a bad placebo alone is insufficient), all targets, matched support, >=3-pair subset, cold strata, other seeds and 2020. Keep negative results and support failures, no relaxing thresholds. Matched sample is selected; training residuals use earlier models and dependencies remain. Pointwise intervals are conditional sensitivity, not joint, simultaneous or causal inference.

A positive result is evidence for additional family-associated information under observed management matching, not genetic inheritance or voluntary niche preference. A null or support failure limits interpretation. Record same-trainer vs actual rearing environment distinction. This does not estimate mediation or infer that family allocation causes weak performance transfer. No wagering or individual records.
