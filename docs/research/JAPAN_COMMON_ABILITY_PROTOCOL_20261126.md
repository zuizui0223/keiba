# Cross-race common ability from the observed opponent network

26 November 2026. Horse-only research, prompted by comparison of two one-win horses meeting for the first time. Freeze before examining new graph scores or subgroup counts. Source A SHA locked, complete classified JRA flat-race fields, 2,092,836 starts/90,962 horses source parity across circuits. Model all classified pairs in each historical race, with logistic P(i ahead of j)=sigmoid(theta_i−theta_j), composite likelihood weights 1/(field−1) times calendar-year exponential recency (half-life 2 years). L2 penalty 1, fixed, optimization convergence checked. Year-start fits on all strictly earlier recorded JRA years; never use current race results or same-year results in rating. Connected components of actual historical opponent graph audited separately: disconnected or unseen horses may be ridge-anchored numerically but do not have data-identified cross-component ability differences. Preserve all comparison denominators, score only historically connected pairs. Scalar context-averaged model, not absolute speed or context-adjusted trait. Baseline uses same earlier records and recency weights, 5 pseudo-starts at mean normalized rank 0.5; logit of shrunk mean (higher better). Both methods calibrate nonnegative logistic slope on 2019 first-meeting connected pairs only, same fixed grid, race-equal Brier, smallest coefficient on ties. Evaluate separately 2020/2021, no tuning on either. Update each year using earlier-year observations only. Primary 2021 first recorded JRA meeting with both horses having exactly one earlier recorded JRA win before target day; general first-meeting comparator. More restrictive both exactly one earlier start and one win, first-meeting naive tie and all-connected comparisons secondary. Prior-day counts and pair first-day identify evaluation strata only, not predictor features; no same-day outcome enters count. Archive may omit earlier career wins, other circuits, nonfinishers, entries, so never equate with official one-win/debut status. Minimum 100 distinct pairs, 30 days for intervals; 999 calendar-day conditional resamples with 97.5% intervals for both primary/general contrasts, other intervals diagnostic. Equal race weighting among selected pairs. Report all outcomes, support, graph components, and rating calibration; do not promote a favorable subgroup if primary fails. Shared graph estimates create dependence not covered by date bootstrap. Graph comparisons are relative statistical ability, not genetic superiority, physical interference, causal race strength, absolute seconds or future betting predictions.
