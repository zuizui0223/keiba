# POH recorded-road-distance sensitivity: matched target and donor comparison

26 November 2026. Secondary sensitivity designed after PR98 and PR99 outcome results, before inspecting road-context fits or distance values. This cannot establish independent replication or identify genetic causation. Same locked Dryad release, POH endpoint, seeds, donor/target assignment, nested 0/1/3 masks, age/month×sex baseline, ridge penalty 1, own shrinkage 3, signed GRM correction and conditional bootstrap/permutation rules as PR98. First reproduce original gain and RMSE within 1e-10 in all seeds. Road predictor is the recorded numeric dist_road column, standardized by donor-only equal-animal-weighted mean and SD per fold, appended linearly to the fixed baseline. No transform search or tuning. No duplicate POH cells expected; fail on drift. Exclude nonfinite or negative road values in both comparison arms only, report denominators, require >=100 targets. If complete, exactly reproduce original cohort. Confirm identical target, history masks, donor labels and outcome before paired contrasts. Refit both baseline and genomic correction on donor residuals when road enters. Thus fixed-correction identity applies within each model, not across models. Report both gain0 and gain3−gain0, 97.5% conditional paired deer intervals for each, all depth gains, sex-blocked 199 label shuffles, and paired road-minus-base differences with conditional 97.5% intervals using same deer. Report constant road values within deer and missingness, not IDs or values. No claim of equivalence if contrast includes zero. Road distance may be concurrent with or partly determined by habitat use: this is an observed-covariate conditional sensitivity only, not independently measured availability, causal control or future forecast. Do not assert that it exhausts spatial confounding, or promote a weaker sign as full replication. Positive and adverse results both retained. A formal trait difference needs same matched deer/periods and common endpoint scales, not the current separate samples. No raw redistribution.
