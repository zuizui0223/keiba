## Manuscript decisions

Core novelty is the combination of fixed-correction within-horse decomposition, direction-only matched alternative-sire diagnostic, and observation membership. The exact squared-loss identity is elementary algebra, not itself a novel mathematical theorem. The application and separation of quantities in the observed data are the contribution. Family resemblance alone, high prediction performance alone, ABM/PDE and market models, anonymous genomes and partial pedigrees are not sufficient to make a single causal paper; those projects remain separate. No pooled meta-analysis across the 18 overlapping analyses. This manuscript can be written before the 2027 study, but submission should not claim a positive prospective result.
