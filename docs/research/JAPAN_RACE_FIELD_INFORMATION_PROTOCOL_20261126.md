No current ranks enter predictor construction; field membership itself is retrospective and may exclude nonfinishers, so do not call it a race-entry forecast. Reuse existing STRONG_NO_KIN / STRONG_KIN prehistory features and append earlier same-context trainer residual mean and log support, excluding focal horse. Ridge alpha 100, horse-equal fit on 2016–18 JRA starts; freeze both fits, calibration 2019 JRA starts; no retraining or tuning on 2020/21. Score raw model, neutral midpoint, linear mean-centered field projection (slope grid 0,.25,.5,.75,1,1.25,1.5,2,3,4, each race capped to keep [0,1]), and pairwise projection q_i = sum_{j!=i} sigmoid(lambda*(s_i-s_j))/(n-1) (grid 0,.5,1,2,3,4,6,8,12,16,24,32,48,64), own/kin models separately. Choose smallest grid parameter minimizing 2019 equal-race MSE. Both projections guarantee rank mean 0.5 in each classified field, and maintain order; nonlinear method depends on distribution of opponents even at fixed mean, not physical interference. Equal-race weight (1/n per start) primary, horse-equal secondary. Primary pooled 2020–21 contrasts: no-kin linear vs pairwise loss, no-kin pairwise vs kin pairwise loss. Positive = improvement. Two 97.5% percentile conditional intervals from 999 whole-calendar-day resamples; per-year diagnostics and raw/linear contrasts descriptive.
