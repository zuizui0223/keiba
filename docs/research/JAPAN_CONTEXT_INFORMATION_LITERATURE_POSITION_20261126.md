Eleven references appear in [v2](JAPAN_CONTEXT_INFORMATION_MANUSCRIPT_V2.md), including the three original foundational references and eight additional references checked against publisher/ bibliographic records. The original research is a retrospective prediction comparison, not genetic-value estimation.
