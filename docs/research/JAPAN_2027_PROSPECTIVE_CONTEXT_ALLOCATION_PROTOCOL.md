# Prospective environmental allocation and predictive information substitution (2027)

**Design frozen 2026-11-25 JST; HOLD_NO_AUTHORIZED_OFFICIAL_PANEL.** This protocol is distinct from the existing 2027 normalized finishing-rank study. It is not a betting model, a download tool, an actual prospective result, or proof of animal learning or genetic adaptation.

## Question and fixed endpoint

Do historically calibrated family corrections improve the prediction of realized environmental allocation when individual history is sparse, including horses not previously observed racing? The primary cohort comprises all horses in an authorized, source-certified, stable-ID JRA registry/eligibility snapshot actually captured and committed before **2027-01-01 00:00 JST**. Include horses with zero observed prior starts and unknown parents; do not choose them using subsequent racing, outcomes, names, or parent availability. Record horses newly registered after the cutoff separately; do not silently add them to the frozen primary cohort. A horse may have prior racing outside the supplied history, so distinguish verified unraced status from zero observed starts.

Primary response: fraction of classified, source-complete JRA flat starts on turf among 1600–1999m turf/dirt starts, 2027-01-01 through 2027-03-27 inclusive. Conditional on >=1 observed eligible start, each horse contributes equal weight. Secondary endpoints fixed in advance: first observed eligible surface in the window, and full-calendar 2027 allocation if complete follow-up becomes available, analyzed separately. Neither is voluntary habitat preference or fitness. Do not use the surface of the first target entry as a feature to predict that entry or the primary fraction. The schedule dates define a proposed window, not a claim about future race schedules.

Use a licensed stable-ID historical panel of entrants, finalized results, and typed direct sire/dam records. Retain registration/eligibility snapshot for the frozen universe; never infer full JRA coverage from a selected set of named horses. Preserve missing, conflicting, non-comparable and unresolved parent IDs as distinct states, not shared phantom parents. Use [official identity protocol](JAPAN_2027_OFFICIAL_ID_ROLLING_FORECAST_PROTOCOL.md), [typed registry bridge](JAPAN_REGISTRY_OBSERVATION_STATE_BRIDGE.md), and [authorized private snapshot capture](JAPAN_2027_JRAVAN_ASOF_CAPTURE_PROTOCOL.md). JRA and NAR identities may not be assumed interchangeable. No bulk scraping of restricted pedigree sites.

All model inputs and historical labels must be actually available in a source-certified snapshot strictly before the model cutoff. Official historical race dates or record MakeDate do not retrospectively establish availability. Use previous full years' classified eligible starts for own counts; family donor residuals for matching Jan1–Mar27 windows. Fit chronological seasonal profiles and count-dependent weights only on completed pre-2027 years, with earlier-year predictors and opposite global horse-half. Retain the original PR93/87 shrinkage, four count bins (0,1–3,4–7,8+), count support gates (>=100 informative horses and >=30 sires per calibration bin per fold), nonnegative bounded decreasing calibration, no-family fallback. Training years must be frozen in the private manifest before data inspection; recommended 2019–24 calibration on an authorized archive with earlier histories and 2025–26 historical validation, **not** a predetermined claim that those data exist. No result-driven relaxation, changed bands or alternate fitting after 2027 outcomes.

## Model contrasts, selection, and support

N = own history + earlier donor global residual; F = N + full same-sire correction; P = N + historically fitted common bounded scalar times same correction; H = full correction only if no eligible prior starts; G = N + historically calibrated history-count-dependent correction. Freeze identical models for all horses; unsupported family correction becomes zero for all four family variants. Primary G versus N, horse-equal MSE gain, positive only if pointwise whole-sire 999-draw interval excludes zero on >=100 horses and >=30 known sire clusters. Report F, P, H contrasts and all count strata, including unknown parent IDs and no-history cohorts. For missing sire, horse remains in all-target denominator, with zero family increment; use unique singleton resampling IDs for unknown-sire horses, report separate known-sire sensitivity and known-sire support. Do not group missing IDs as one family. Confirm calibration support and all cohort gates before scoring; if not, HOLD. Same split seeds as PR87, one primary, other seeds dependent sensitivity. No claims of independent replications or simultaneous inference.

Track every frozen horse: no observed eligible start, observed eligible start with strict classified data, eligible but missing/nonclassified outcomes, unsupported parent/identity, or source coverage unresolved. Report the complete observed candidate race denominator, not just matched runners. If full eligible-start observation is not verified, state the conditional strict-observation estimand and missingness limitations rather than treat no observation as zero starts. Separately report outside-universe registered-after-cutoff and unjoined records, without predicting them retrospectively. Conditional pointwise clusters do not capture all race/management/deeper-pedigree dependence. New entrant/cold-start results are descriptive subgroups, not proof of interaction, unless a separate prospective interaction estimand is fixed in advance.

## Stop rule

The strongest current historical evidence concerns information substitution and recorded-parent alignment; 2024 primary gated transfer remains uncertain, longer distance weaker, and race-supply balance unsupported. PR93's 2025 partial-season extension is running at this protocol snapshot, not used to select a favorable design. Prospective evaluation requires authorized stable-ID data and a distinct real pre-window capture/commit. Do not claim a 2027 result, or that animal cognition, genetic inheritance, true feasible choice, or adaptive causation has been observed. This document establishes a design only.
