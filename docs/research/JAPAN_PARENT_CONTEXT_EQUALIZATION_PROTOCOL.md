# Parent-channel environmental allocation with equal observed peer counts

2026-11-24. Follow-up to PR82. Father-linked history robustly predicts the first observed eligible surface, especially in history-poor strata. Earlier relative-rank analyses found many more paternal than maternal peers. Test whether observed environmental allocation information is asymmetric after equalizing recorded peer counts, not genetic contribution.

## Fixed design

Exact source A SHA, original 2,092,836 starts/90,962 horses, PR82 annual denominator and its primary C−F score must reproduce before new contrasts. Retain first eligible JRA turf/dirt 1600–1999m annual outcome, unchanged strong baseline and opposite horse-half year-forward fit, alpha=100. Direct recorded parents only; missing dam remains a zero-count channel. Build earlier-year opposite-half donor profiles by equal unique-horse means. Counts n_s,n_d and k=min(n_s,n_d). Rank eligible donor IDs by SHA of fixed split seed, year, half, draw, side, parent, donor ID; take first k, no outcome-based sampling. Eight fixed draws 0–7, same sample for each parent/half/year/k. Signal = sum of centered observed first-surface fractions divided by count+8 (minimum one rather than three for this new paired design, fixed before results). Full and equalized features include same own-history interactions. Common comparator C includes strong baseline plus log1p(n_s,n_d,k). FS/FD add full father/mother signal, ES/ED add equal-count father/mother signal, EB both equal-count signals. Refit each model separately; use all annual rows for learning, report all and supported cohorts. Zero support remains in all-target denominator; models can differ there due to re-estimation.

**Primary** 2021, first seed, k>=1, ED−ES paired mean squared loss (positive favors father). Also C−ES, C−ED, C−EB, full FD−FS, and both-signal increments. Average losses over the eight fixed draws before summaries (not ensemble prediction). Report 2020, other two seeds, all rows, k>=3, cold strata, support and draw-specific direct contrast. Equal number is not equal depth, genetic relatedness or environmental precision. Separate 999-draw father and mother cluster intervals (minimum 30 groups); pointwise conditional sensitivity, not joint or multiplicity-adjusted. A directional channel advantage is tentative if either interval includes zero or sensitivity reverses; no claim of genetic sex-specific inheritance. Missing parents counted explicitly. All outcomes retained.

Prior summaries use earlier years; entry age/sex/trainer are recorded conditional covariates, not verified available before decisions. Data are retrospective; repeated historical records and opposite-half features remain dependent. No biological causation, voluntary habitat preference, official debut, market or betting inference. No individual outputs.
