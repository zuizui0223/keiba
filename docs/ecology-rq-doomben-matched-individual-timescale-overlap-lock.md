# Same-horse matched-context bridge between Doomben within-2025 and 2025→2026 checks

**Post-hoc ecological identifiability protocol fixed 2026-10-15 BEFORE the first
source-read of this new paired-timescale overlap.** Previous within-2025
positive source-conditional score (23 HORSES IDs/24 later rows)
and previous 2025→2026 unfavorable year-forward score
(26 HORSES IDs/27 held-out rows) were ALREADY known. The present
new question is explicitly exploratory, not preregistered.

## Biological identification question

Does an animal that had its own past history tested against later
2025 races **also** have its original HORSES-ID timing history
tested against 2026 observations under an EXACTLY IDENTICAL first
GPS marker and original official race distance? Until both
time-horizon groups are defined on the SAME individual and
measurement context, comparing their source-conditional RMSE
does not identify within-individual temporal decay.

Use ONLY original prelocked **23 Doomben 2025 + 19 Doomben 2026**
dates (42 original dates total) and numbered races 1–6,
as defined before both original analyses. No adding dates or
altering marker or distance after seeing overlap.

Data are same first-party Racing Queensland positive-GPS times
uniquely linked in memory to numeric HORSES IDs, screened by
the previously used source identity consistency and at least
four positive first-GPS horses per original race. Missing dates
are source HOLD, not animals with zero performance. Original
per-source date/race/GPS/horse roster SHA256 and anonymity rules
stay intact.

## Two FIXED separately scored histories, same original individuals

A. Within-2025: source scored focal horse records after a strictly
earlier 2025 record, compared on the 2025 future observations with
other original horses' earlier 2025 records. Use the same
fixed n/(n+2) shrinkage, same relative initial first-GPS timing,
same race-distance and marker definition, and existing
source data source_timed_panel checks.

B. Forward-2026: train only with ALL 2025 same HORSES-ID early
timing records, score the original 2026 matched-marker-and-distance
records without any 2026 update; compare the identical 2026
future records with other-horse 2025 same-context history,
also with the previously unchanged n/(n+2) shrinkage.

Use the exact **150m** first GPS marker for the primary fixed
question, and exact official race distance. Neither a
first-GPS interval marker nor original race distance may
be harmonized or interpolated. Additional identical Class
is an anonymous support-count-only stricter screen.

Find the intersection of the two groups on
(original numeric HORSES ID, first marker, original race distance).
A horse appearing only at different distances in the two
score groups is NOT an eligible paired-timescale animal.
Report counts of distinct original matched HORSES IDs,
contexts, future observations in 2025/2026 and source dates
represented. Do not report animal identities, event
pairings or individual errors.

The same horse ID might have multiple target observations in
each group. To avoid overweighting a prolific individual,
within each matched HORSES ID first average squared errors
across its 2025 future rows (or 2026 future rows), then average
across the SAME matched HORSES ID population, then square root
for RMSE. Do not count repeated rows as additional independent
animals. Report positive/negative own-vs-other point gain separately
for 2025 and 2026, NOT their difference as a time effect.

## Strict numerical support and publication gate

Before any matched-subset RMSE is promoted, require ALL:
- at least 20 distinct original HORSES IDs in both time-score groups
  **with the same exact marker and distance**
- at least 20 scored 2025 future rows **and** 20 scored 2026
  future rows, measured from those same IDs/contexts
- at least 6 original 2025 future meeting dates and
  at least 3 original 2026 future meeting dates
- at least 20 original HORSES-ID × marker × distance strata.

If any fails, return only **HOLD_INSUFFICIENT_SAME_HORSE_TWO_TIMESCALE_CONTEXT_SUPPORT**
and anonymous support counts. Do not publish an unsupported
descriptive RMSE, difference, null, p-value or CI. These are
heuristic engineering support criteria, not power.

The distinct 2025 original and 2026 original date coverage must
also be reported. A supported same-ID numerical contrast would
remain post-hoc source-conditional because changing source year
also changes peer field, physical condition, racing class and
trainer/rider histories. This analysis **does not randomize time**,
and no causal aging/decay, inherited personality, adaptation,
ground-reaction H1 or opponent H2 is identified.

The primary scientific result may legitimately be a support HOLD:
that would demonstrate that the existing two positive/negative
source-cohort scores cannot be interpreted as a within-animal
timescale contrast.

## Privacy/source rights

No output of original horse ID/name, numeric jockey/trainer licence,
per-horse matched measurement, exact individual GPS split or event
pairing; no betting or horse-specific prediction. Only anonymous
source-date hashes, sample counts, scientific gate codes and
**supported** cohort aggregate score summaries may be saved.
This remains Racing Queensland public original data with
its attribution/reuse constraints.
